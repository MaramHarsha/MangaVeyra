What only
MangaVeyra does

Every panel is structured data, not a flat picture. That is what makes these possible.

  • Consistency

    Character Lock

    Train a character once. Every panel gets a score; drifted faces are flagged and fixed by regenerating just that character.

  • Editing

    Fix one part

    Change a hand, a sign or one panel. Everything outside your selection stays pixel-for-pixel the same.

  • Models

    Auto-routing

    Each job goes to the best model you have: local GPU, OpenRouter, fal or a direct key. The reason is always shown.

  • Layers

    Layered panels

    Background, each character, effects and lettering regenerate separately.

  • Reading Soon

    Panel-aware reader

    Guided panel-by-panel view on phones, with alt-text and read-aloud written from the script.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The open-source edition is the full studio, reader and community under AGPL-3.0. A hosted option may come later through World; it will not remove anything from the free one.

MangaVeyra is pre-alpha: the studio and exports work today, and publishing, the reader and the community are being built now.

Do I need a GPU?

No. Every flow works without one: the built-in placeholder model makes labelled placeholder art, and you can connect OpenAI, OpenRouter or fal with your own keys. A GPU worker is optional; it runs local image models and optional character training on your own hardware.

Which AI models does it support?

For scripts: OpenAI, OpenRouter, or a local OpenAI-compatible server such as Ollama or LM Studio. For art: OpenAI GPT Image, OpenRouter and fal, or diffusers and ComfyUI models on your own GPU worker. Auto-routing picks a model for each job and shows why. Local only mode guarantees that no prompt or image leaves your server.

Who owns what I make?

You do. Your projects live in your own database and storage, and MangaVeyra claims no rights over them: the AGPL-3.0 covers the software, not your stories. The licences of the models you connect still apply, and the licence guard can keep models without commercial permission off public series.

How are AI-made pages labelled for readers?

Every chapter carries a label for each role, script, art and lettering: human, AI-assisted or AI. When publishing arrives (it is being built now), each published chapter keeps those labels, and readers see them on the series page and in the reader.
